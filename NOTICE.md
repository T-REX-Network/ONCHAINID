## ONCHAINID Smart Contracts

Copyright (C) 2026 Digital Asset Operational Services ISAC Ltd. ("T-REX Network")

ONCHAINID is the open-source digital identity infrastructure of the T-REX
ecosystem for compliant tokenized assets. It implements the ERC-734 (key
holder) and ERC-735 (claim holder) standards on a modular ERC-7579 smart
account with ERC-4337 account abstraction support.
